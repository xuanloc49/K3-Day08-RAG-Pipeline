## Citing and referencing
Learn the difference between citing and referencing, why it matters and correctly cite all your information sources in a reference list.

This resource is essential to your studies and also explains why you need to reference. The link leads to the RMIT Australia library. 
[Referencing ](https://www.rmit.edu.au/library/study/referencing)

Easy Cite is a reference tool that lets you look up referencing tips and examples in a selection of common styles used at RMIT.

### Need help with finding information for your assignment?
When you contact us, we will:
  * **Ask** you questions about what you are studying, your assignment question, and what search strategies you’ve already tried if you are looking for information.
  * **Advise** you on effective search strategy to help you find relevant and quality information for your assignment.
  * **Refer** you to other teams in the Library, or elsewhere in RMIT, if we cannot help you directly.


#### Liaison librarians can help to
  * Find materials for your assessments
  * Evaluate your sources 
  * Assist with citing and referencing and how to use referencing tools
  * Help you understand how to use generative AI tools in your learning and how to evaluate AI-generated content.
